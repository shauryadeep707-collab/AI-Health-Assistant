How AI Health Assistant Works

1

Describe Symptoms

Type naturally in plain English, Hindi, or Hinglish. Our conversational engine listens and asks targeted, clinical follow-ups.

2

Automated Talk Summary

The dialogue is instantly synthesized into a structured clinical summary detailing chief complaint, severity, temperature, and medications.

3

Auto-Routing to Specialist

No patient clicks needed: your case and summary are automatically routed to the best-matching verified doctor's queue.

Integrated Healthcare Architecture

🚨

Instant Emergency Red-Flag Override

Critical symptoms (e.g. crushing chest pain, severe dyspnea) bypass questionnaire wait times, trigger direct 112/108 routing, and alert doctors even offline.

📍

Geolocation & Real-time Map

Locates verified, practicing physicians around Dehradun and Uttarakhand using Haversine great-circle calculation and interactive Leaflet maps.

📋

Automated Clinical Dialogue Briefing

Doctors receive structured triage briefs with chief complaints, progression, and complete dialogue transcripts, eliminating redundant patient questioning.

📶

Offline PWA & Background Sync

Works seamlessly without active internet connectivity. Messages and emergency requests queue locally in IndexedDB and synchronize automatically upon reconnection.
