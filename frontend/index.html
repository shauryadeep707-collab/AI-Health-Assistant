How It Works

1

Describe Symptoms

Type your concerns naturally in English, Hindi, or Hinglish. The AI asks smart, conversational follow-ups.

2

Risk Assessment

Hard safety rules plus AI scoring classify symptoms into Low, Medium, or Urgent risk levels.

3

Doctor Connect & Care

Low risk gets safe OTC guidance; Medium risk connects you directly to available local doctors with an AI summary.

Comprehensive Healthcare Support

🚨

Instant Emergency Override

Red-flag symptoms (chest pain, breathing trouble, severe bleeding) trigger instant 112/108 routing, fully functional even offline.

📍

Geolocation & Interactive Map

Locates verified, practicing physicians around Dehradun using Haversine calculation and OpenStreetMap.

📋

Doctor Case Briefing (FIFO Queue)

Doctors receive concise, under-120-word clinical summaries, saving consultation time and prioritizing care.

📶

PWA & Offline IndexedDB Outbox

Works seamlessly without internet. Messages and consultation requests queue locally and sync automatically when online.
